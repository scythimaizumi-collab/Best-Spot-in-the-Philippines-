About the Place

A small island with a world-famous shore

Boracay is a tiny island off the northwestern tip of Panay in Aklan. Its four-kilometer White Beach is regularly ranked among the best beaches on the planet — soft, powdery sand that stays cool underfoot, and shallow turquoise water perfect for swimming. After a landmark rehabilitation in 2018, the island reopened cleaner, greener, and more sustainable than ever.

Attractions

Sights you can't miss

Beyond the beach, Boracay is full of iconic viewpoints and natural wonders.

White Beach

The island's crown jewel — four kilometers of powdery white sand divided into three stations.

Puka Beach

A quieter northern beach known for its crushed-puka-shell sand and relaxed vibe.

Mount Luho Viewpoint

The highest point on the island, offering a panoramic 360° view of Boracay.

Activities

Experiences you shouldn't miss

From lazy beach days to golden-hour sails, Boracay rewards every kind of traveler.

Island Hopping

Cruise on a traditional banca to Puka Beach, Crocodile Island, and hidden snorkeling coves.

Paraw Sailing at Sunset

Ride a native double-outrigger sailboat and watch the sky turn gold over the horizon.

Water Sports

Paddleboarding, kite surfing at Bulabog Beach, helmet diving, and reef snorkeling.

Food and Culture

Eat with your hands, feast like family

Boracay's food scene blends fresh island seafood with Filipino tradition. Try a kamayan feast — grilled fish, crabs, and shrimp piled on banana leaves and eaten by hand. Local delicacies like chori burger, fresh mango shakes, and seafood paluto (cook-your-own-catch) are island staples. The warm, welcoming culture of the Aklanons makes every meal feel like a celebration.

Travel Guide

How to get there

Fly into Caticlan (MPH) or Kalibo (KLO) airport, then take a short tricycle or van ride to the Caticlan Jetty Port. From there, a 10–15 minute ferry brings you to Boracay, followed by a quick ride to your resort. The best time to visit is November to May, during the dry season, when the seas are calm and the skies are clear.

Accommodations

Where to stay

Boracay offers everything from budget-friendly hostels to luxury beachfront resorts. Station 1 is quiet and upscale with the finest sand, Station 2 is the lively center of dining and nightlife, and Station 3 is relaxed and budget-friendly. Most resorts are certified sustainable businesses under the island's new eco-tourism rules.

Events

Celebrations on the island

Boracay comes alive with festivals throughout the year. The Boracay International Funboard Cup draws kite surfers each January, while summer brings beach parties, sandcastle competitions, and the famous Ati-Atihan-inspired street dances. Every evening, sunset gatherings along White Beach turn the shoreline into a daily celebration.
